Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026 · Lucky Tarot: Love & Money

This Privacy Policy explains how Maksym Tereshchenko, an individual developer based in Ukraine (“we”, “us”), collects and uses information when you use the Lucky Tarot app and website (the “Service”). Contact: tarotsupports@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Profile you provide: first name, date of birth, the topics you choose during onboarding, and your language.
  • Content you create: questions you ask, your tarot readings and messages you send to the AI Oracle.
  • App identifiers: a random identifier generated on your device so we can keep your profile without an email or password. If you use Lucky Tarot inside Telegram, we receive your Telegram user ID, first name and language from Telegram.
  • Purchase information: subscription status and transaction identifiers provided by Apple / Google through our payment processor. We never receive your card details.
  • Usage events: basic in-app events (for example, “opened the card of the day”) used to improve the product.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifier (IDFA).

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: calculate your sign and numbers, draw cards, and personalise readings.
  • To generate AI responses: your question, the cards drawn and basic astrology data (sign, life path number, moon phase) are sent to our AI provider to create the text.
  • To manage subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To send the optional daily reminder (scheduled on your device; you can turn it off at any time).
  • To keep the Service secure and to understand how features are used.

3. Service providers

  • Anthropic — AI text generation (readings, Oracle chat, horoscopes).
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google — subscription purchases and status.
  • Railway — hosting of our servers and database.
  • Telegram — only if you open Lucky Tarot inside Telegram.

These providers process data only to deliver their services to us. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in Profile → Delete account. Deletion is permanent. An active App Store subscription must be cancelled separately in your device settings.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain processing. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Age

The Service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you in the app of material changes.
